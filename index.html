What people ask

Is this just another chatbot?
No. A chatbot replies. These are AI workers that act — they answer calls, route requests, schedule, follow up, and report back. They execute work, they don't just hold a conversation.
Do we have to replace the tools we use?
Not necessarily. The audit maps what you already run before we change anything. We build around your workflow — not over it.
What about our data and privacy?
Your data stays yours. Access is scoped to the work, nothing is trained on your customer data, and billing keeps human review. Security is designed in, not bolted on.
How fast can this be running?
Audit within days. Pilot runs on your real data for 3 days. A retained build is sized to your workflow — you see it working before you commit.
What if we don't like it?
Cancel — no contract, no penalty. You're out $500 and I'm out of your hair. I don't like dealing with contracts either.
Who do you work with?
Field service first — HVAC & plumbing, electrical & trades, roofing & restoration, multi-location service teams. If missed calls and handoffs cost you work, that's our lane.
