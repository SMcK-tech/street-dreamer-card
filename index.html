What owners ask

Is this just another chatbot?
No. A chatbot replies — it doesn't act. These workers qualify, schedule, follow up until it closes or dies, and report back. A chatbot can't book a 2am emergency install or recover a quote that went silent three weeks ago.
My techs won't use it.
It works for the tech, not against them. It routes the right job to the right tech the first time, books the work, follows up with customers, and takes the admin off their plate. Less phone tag, less paperwork, more billable hours. If it didn't make their day easier, it wouldn't survive the pilot.
We've tried software before.
Software puts work on you — you have to run it, log into it, babysit it. This is a hire that takes work off you. Nothing to configure, no dashboard to watch; the worker does the job and tells you what happened. You'll feel the difference inside the first 3-day pilot.
What about our data?
Your data stays yours. Access is scoped to the work, nothing is trained on your customer data, and a human reviews anything sensitive. We work inside your existing stack — you keep what you run.
What if we don't like it?
Cancel. Month to month, no contract, no penalty. Worst case you're out $500 and you finally know exactly what's sitting in your voicemail. I'd rather earn it than lock you in.
Who do you work with?
Veteran-owned residential HVAC companies in the South — Birmingham first, then all of Alabama, then out. One lane, done properly. If you've got trucks, techs, and a phone that stops ringing at 5pm, that's my lane.
